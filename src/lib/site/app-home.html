        Why this exists

        I built this because I got tired of paying eleven companies to give me half a sales process.

        Jared Janacek Founder · Global Contractor Network RRCA · Roof King Coating · Buddy's Roofing · GCN Equipment

        Fifteen years in roofing. Door to door, storm work, insurance restoration, and now commercial coatings. I have hired green reps and watched them quit in six weeks because the job was actually twelve jobs: measure it, price it, write it, chase it, file it, order it.

        Every tool I bought solved one of those twelve and created a new gap on either side of itself. The CRM didn't know what the estimator priced. The estimator didn't know what the supplier charged this week. The proposal tool didn't know the job existed. And none of them told a new guy what to say when the property manager said "just email me something."

        So I built the whole line. One login, one price book, one record of the job from the first pin on the map to the final invoice. The estimating engine is the one I use on my own commercial work — production rates, coating stacks, soft costs, the lot — not a simplified version made for a demo.

        If it doesn't close deals, it doesn't ship. That's the only test I hold it to.
