    The nine minutes

    An inspection that a rookie cannot get wrong.

    The app tells him which photo comes next, which slope he is standing on and what he still owes the file. He is never asked to remember a protocol he learned once in a Tuesday meeting.

    Guided photo protocol

    Shot by shot, in the order a carrier expects it.

    • Elevations, slopes, test squares, penetrations
    • Every photo stamped with slope and time
    • Missing shots block the report, not the adjuster

    Trace and measure

    Draw the roof line on imagery and get the numbers immediately.

    • Area, perimeter, ridge, hip, valley, rake, eave
    • Pitch estimate per slope
    • Waste factor applied on the spot

    Damage capture

    Hail hits, wind creasing, granule loss, mat exposure — tagged where they are.

    • Test square counts by slope
    • Collateral: soft metals, screens, AC fins
    • Notes attach to the photo, not a legal pad
