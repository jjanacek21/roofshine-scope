# Put Company Training in Company Resources

## Change
- Add **Company Training** as its own navigation item directly beneath **Survival Guide** in the desktop **Resources** section.
- Add the same item directly beneath **Survival Guide** in the mobile **Resources** menu so placement is consistent on phones and tablets.
- Link the item to the existing company classroom at `/cb/training`.
- Remove **Company Training** and **Manage courses** from the Survival Guide page header; the training module will no longer appear inside the Survival Guide screen.
- Keep Company Training removed from the Claim Buddy dashboard and Claim Buddy admin tabs.

## Access
- Team members enter through **Resources → Company Training**.
- Existing owner/admin course-management functionality remains available from the training module; no training data, permissions, or features will be changed.
